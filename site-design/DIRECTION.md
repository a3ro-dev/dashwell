# Dashwell site: direction

## Brief (self-authored from the conversation, 2026-11-18)
- What: a GitHub Pages site for Dashwell, a browser extension + Android app that fixes the Tumblr dashboard.
- Who: people who live on their Tumblr dash, mostly fandom folks, tired of ads, an algorithm that floods them after one like, and missing posts from mutuals.
- One job: in ten seconds understand "one like won't flood your feed anymore, ads gone, mutuals never missed", then get the extension or the APK.
- Feel: clean, minimal, peak. References: (assumed) a quiet well, a sticker zine, a mixing desk.
- Exists: product, real screenshots, real copy from the app, user's voice skill (lower-case, plain). New logo needed. No paid image generation.
- Copy voice: a3ro-dev posting register: lower-case, plain, honest, no em dashes, no slogans.

## Category default refused
Extension landing pages share the Launch Page face: dark ground, centred headline, tilted screenshot with glow, three icon feature cards, "Add to Chrome" button, FAQ. Tumblr's own cliché is navy #001935 + Tumblr blue + Favorit. Also refused: the Paper Edition (warm paper, serif with one italic green word, pill badge, mono eyebrows).

## Three concepts
- A (nature): Dashwell is a well. Dark water ground, serif-less humanist, ripples drawn in canvas. Accent: lamp yellow.
- B (play): Dashwell is a sticker sheet. Hot pink colour field, rounded heavy type, tags as die-cut stickers that peel and fold. Accent: black ink on pink.
- C (media): Dashwell is a mixing desk. Light ground, condensed grotesque, one fader per topic rebalancing live as a feed scrolls. Accent: signal orange.

## Pick: A, the well (with C's fair-share idea in the copy)
- A won: calm and clean like the brief asks, and its evidence is the real product line ("you've seen a lot of #visionquest lately · show") sinking into the water. It also gives the logo its meaning: a ring (the well) with a dash at the water line.
- B lost: memorable and very tumblr, but loud; a pink field fights the "clean, minimal" ask and Tumblr's own palettes.
- C lost: the faders explain the algorithm instead of showing the product; abstract. Its fair-share idea survives as copy.
- History: site|nature|dark|grotesque|yellow|shape|scroll-story.

## Tokens
- ground #07131a (deep well water), raised #0f2028, line #ffffff1a; ink #e9f1f2, ink-2 #9fb4b8 (tinted from the water); accent lamp #f4d35e, one job: the primary action and the logo's dash.
- type: Bricolage Grotesque (opsz axis: tight, characterful at display sizes, plain at text sizes; lower-case copy needs a face with real x-height). Scale 15 / 17 / 21 / 32 / 56 / clamp(44,6.4vw,92).
- radius family: 12 (buttons, cards), 24 (phone shots). Max width 1200, margins clamp(20px,5vw,64px).

## Richness
Shape: concentric ripples drawn live in canvas (slow, pauses off-screen, still frame is beautiful), plus real product footage: a raea-rendered loop of a feed where the third #visionquest post folds away, and real phone screenshots.

## Grammar (scroll-story, five sections)
1. what is this, can i get it: headline, lede, two buttons, the well.
2. does it actually work: the raea feed loop + the fixes as plain type.
3. what does it look like on my phone: two real screenshots.
4. how does it decide: a short honest paragraph.
5. how do i install it: android + browser steps, then footer (privacy, terms, licence, not affiliated).

## Signature
A post card drops into the well, ripples spread from where it lands, and it settles as one calm line with "show". 6s loop, ease-in-out; reduced motion: the card sits still, rings static.

## Voice
a3ro-dev posting register: lower-case, plain, a bit funny, no em dashes, no slogans.
