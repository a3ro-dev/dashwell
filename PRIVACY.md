# Privacy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Dashwell (the browser extension and the Android app) collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no server of ours.

## What stays on your device

To keep your feed varied, Dashwell remembers:

- the tags and blogs on posts you scroll past, like and reblog, with times, so it can tell what you've seen a lot of and what you love;
- your favorite blogs, muted words and settings;
- the newest posts from your favorite blogs that you haven't seen.

This lives only in your browser's extension storage, or in the Android app's private storage. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

**Delete it any time:** tap **Start over** in Dashwell's settings, or remove the extension or uninstall the app.

## Network requests Dashwell makes

Dashwell talks to exactly these places, and sends nothing about your interests to any of them:

| To | Why | What they can see |
|---|---|---|
| `tumblr.com` | You're using Tumblr. Dashwell only changes how its pages look on your device. | Same as without Dashwell. Tumblr's [privacy policy](https://www.tumblr.com/privacy) applies. |
| `<blog>.tumblr.com/rss` | Checks your favorite blogs for new posts (every 30 minutes in the extension, when you open settings in the app). Plain public feeds, no login. | That someone at your IP address read that blog's public feed. |
| `api.github.com` and `github.com` | Checks for a newer Dashwell (the app at most once a day, the extension when you open settings) and downloads updates when you ask. | Your IP address and app version. GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies. |

Dashwell also **blocks** requests from tumblr.com pages to known ad and tracking networks. That's what the ad blocker is.

## Your Tumblr login (Android app)

You log in on Tumblr's own login page inside the app. Dashwell never sees, stores or sends your password. Tumblr's session cookie is kept in the app's private storage, like any browser keeps it, so you stay logged in across updates.

## Children

Dashwell has no account and collects no personal data, from anyone. Tumblr's own age rules apply to using Tumblr.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the release notes will say so before the version that changes it ships. Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/a3ro-dev/dashwell/issues).
